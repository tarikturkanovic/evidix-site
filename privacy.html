Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what Evidix AI collects when you use evidixai.com and what we do with it.

What we collect

Account data: your email address and an encrypted password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase).

Your content: the video ideas, settings and any reference images you submit, and the plans generated for you. These are saved to your private project list.

Usage data: your plan, how many generations you have used, and your subscription status.

Payment data: payments are handled by Stripe. We receive your Stripe customer ID, subscription status and plan, but never your full card number.

How we use it

To run the Service: sign you in, generate and save your plans, enforce plan limits, process subscriptions and respond to support requests. We do not sell your personal information and do not use your content for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers, who process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: database and authentication.
  • Vercel: website hosting and serverless functions.
  • Stripe: payments and billing.
  • Anthropic: AI model that writes your plan. The idea and settings you submit are sent to it for each generation.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Access to your data

Your projects are private to your account. Access to data is restricted by per-user security rules in our database.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and projects until you ask us to delete them. You can request access to, correction of or deletion of your data at any time; contact us using the details below and we will act within a reasonable time. Billing records may be kept as long as the law requires.

Cookies and local storage

We use only what is needed to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Contact

Contact us at the support address shown on your Stripe receipt, or through the billing portal.
