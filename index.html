Questions

Good questions

Does Evidix AI make the video?

Evidix AI plans your video and writes the prompts. On a paid plan you can also render each part on the site with Seedance 2.5, paid for with your credits. Or copy the prompts into any video generator you like. AI video varies from run to run, so results are not guaranteed.

Is this affiliated with Higgsfield?

No. Evidix AI is an independent product. We write prompts designed to work well with Higgsfield, but we have no partnership with them. Rendering on the site uses their service and is paid for with Evidix credits.

Do I need to know how to write prompts?

No. You describe your idea in plain language and choose a few settings such as style, duration and mood. Evidix AI does the structuring.

What about longer videos?

Longer ideas are split into connected parts, each with its own prompt, so you can generate them one segment at a time and keep characters and settings consistent.

Can I use it right now?

Yes. Choose a plan at evidixai.com/app and you can generate your first video plan in a minute. Every plan includes monthly credits, detailed prompts and video rendering.

Will the video look exactly like the plan?

No planning tool can promise that. AI video generators vary from run to run. Clear prompts, consistent character descriptions and reference images improve your odds, and that is what Evidix AI focuses on.
