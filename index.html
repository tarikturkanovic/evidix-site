Questions

Questions sellers ask first.

What does Evidix AI do?

It helps you work disputes, returns and chargebacks. It builds the evidence packet for each one, drafts your response, tracks the deadline and follows the case to a decision.

Which marketplaces does it work with?

Evidix AI builds the packet from what you give it and the kind of dispute. It does not connect to your shop, so you stay in charge of what you share. Always check the marketplace's own requirements before you submit.

Does it send anything without me?

No. Evidix AI never contacts a platform or a buyer for you. You review each draft, edit it and send it yourself.

Can it promise I will win a dispute?

No. The platform or the card network makes the decision. Evidix AI makes sure your side is complete, on time and clearly presented. We do not promise a result.

Do I have to give you my password?

No. We never ask for your marketplace password, and Evidix AI does not connect to your shop. You choose what to upload for each case.

How do I get started?

Subscribe for $49 a month and you can add your first dispute right away. There is no call to book.

Is it worth it for a small shop?

If you handle a few disputes, returns or chargebacks every month and each one eats an evening, it is worth a conversation. If you almost never have one, it probably is not for you yet.

Will Evidix AI see my customers' information?

Only what you add to a case. Please leave out anything a dispute does not need, and do not send customer information through this website or by email.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time from your account and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
